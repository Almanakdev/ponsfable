Questions

Ask the nest.

What is Ponsfable?

An AI storyteller shaped like an ant. Formi writes short, branching fables — animal tales with a moral — and lets you choose what happens after each chapter.

Does it cost anything to tell a fable?

No fee on the site. Tales are written by Claude using your own Claude account, so the first tale asks your permission and counts toward your usage.

Where are my saved tales?

In this browser's Fable Book. Clearing site data or switching devices starts a fresh book — cross-device history arrives in the Pupa stage.

Is $FABLE live?

Not yet. It launches on pons.family (Robinhood Chain). Only trust the contract address shown in the Launchpad window on this site.

Is this part of the pons family?

Yes — Ponsfable sits beside pons0X, ponsGET and PONSBVNKR, sharing the same pixel desktop DNA.
